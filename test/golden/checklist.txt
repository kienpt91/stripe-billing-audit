Billing reliability checklist: what the Stripe API cannot show, to check by hand

Failed payments
  [ ] Retry schedule and the state after the last retry
      Dashboard, Billing, Revenue recovery, Retries. Smart Retries (or a custom schedule) is on, the
      number of retries and the window suit your product, and the setting for what happens to the
      subscription when the last retry fails (cancel, mark unpaid, leave past due) matches how your
      application treats access. Retry settings cannot be read through the API.

  [ ] Failed-payment and renewal emails
      Dashboard, Settings, Billing, Subscriptions and emails. Emails for failed payments, upcoming
      renewals and expiring cards are on or replaced by your own, and each carries a link where the
      customer can update the card (a Billing portal session). Email settings cannot be read through
      the API.

Webhooks
  [ ] Webhook handler quality
      In your code, not in Stripe: the Stripe-Signature header is verified against the raw body with
      the endpoint's signing secret, the handler answers 2xx fast and does the work asynchronously,
      processing is idempotent on event.id, out-of-order delivery is tolerated (fetch the current
      object instead of trusting the payload), and events can be replayed after an outage.

  [ ] Alerts for failing webhook endpoints
      Dashboard, Developers, Webhooks. Email alerts for failing endpoints go to a mailbox somebody
      reads, and someone owns the response.

Application
  [ ] Access follows subscription state
      The application grants and removes access from subscription status (active, trialing,
      past_due, unpaid, canceled, paused) according to a written policy, and a scheduled
      reconciliation compares your records with the Stripe API so a missed webhook cannot leave a
      customer with the wrong access for long.

Retention
  [ ] Win-back after a cancellation
      A message or offer goes out when a cancellation is scheduled (customer.subscription.updated
      with cancel_at_period_end) and after it takes effect (customer.subscription.deleted), and the
      cancellation reasons collected by the Billing portal are read by someone.

API
  [ ] API version pinned in code
      The SDK and the Stripe-Version header your application sends are pinned and upgraded on
      purpose, with a test against the new version before the account default or a webhook endpoint
      is moved.

Security
  [ ] Keys and dashboard access
      Each service uses its own restricted key with the permissions it needs, no secret key is in a
      client or a repository, keys are rotated on a schedule and when people leave, two-step
      verification is required for every dashboard user, and roles follow least privilege.

Testing
  [ ] Lifecycle tested with test clocks
      Trial end, successful and failed renewals, upgrades with proration and cancellation have been
      exercised in test mode with test clocks and the webhook handler observed through each of them.

Payments
  [ ] Authentication-required payments
      Off-session renewals that need 3D Secure move the invoice to a state your application handles
      (invoice.payment_action_required), and customers receive a link to authenticate. Radar rules
      do not block legitimate renewals.

Customer communication
  [ ] Receipts, invoices and branding
      Receipts and invoice emails are on, carry the support contact and legal details of the
      business, and the invoice footer and tax IDs are filled in for the countries you invoice.

Disputes
  [ ] Dispute handling
      charge.dispute.created reaches a human who submits evidence before the deadline, and refunds
      follow a written policy.
